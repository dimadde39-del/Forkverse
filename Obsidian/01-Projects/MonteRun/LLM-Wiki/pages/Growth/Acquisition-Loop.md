# Acquisition Loop

## Current Synthesis

MonteRun acquisition should sell truth and clarity, not comedy for its own sake. The first surface should communicate that MonteRun is a runway and survival engine: math decides, levers guide, voice delivers.

## Landing Promise

MonteRun shows how long the user can really last when bad months and delayed income are modeled without copium.

## MVP Acquisition Blocks

- Hero with core promise and CTA.
- Short explanation of Math decides / Levers guide / Voice delivers.
- Example result with base vs stress.
- Why this is not a normal calculator.
- CTA to try the web app or Telegram.

## Channel Assumptions

- X/Twitter: viral cards and discussions.
- Reddit: pain validation and controversial cases.
- Telegram: low-friction retention for part of the audience.

## Agent Caution

Do not let acquisition copy claim guaranteed outcomes, investment advice, or AI prediction. The offer is calculation and scenario clarity.
